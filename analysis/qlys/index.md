## What Qualys is

Qualys sells a cloud platform that finds security weaknesses in a customer's computers, cloud services and applications, ranks them by risk and, increasingly, fixes them. The company says more than 10,000 organizations use it, including most of the Fortune 100. The pitch this year is automated repair: management says customers deployed more than 180 million patches through the platform in the past year, 60% of them autonomously.

The sales mix is moving. Channel partners produced 55% of third quarter revenue, up from 50% a year earlier, and channel revenue grew 24% while direct revenue fell 2%. The quarter was also what management called an unprecedented federal quarter, and it said a few federal deals could have slipped into the fourth quarter. Net dollar expansion, the growth in revenue from existing customers, was 105% and unchanged from the second quarter. That is a loyal base, not a fast-expanding one.

## Capital return

Qualys returns cash through buybacks, not a regular payout. In nine months it spent $185.6 million, which is 74% of free cash flow, and about $176 million remains authorized. The test of this policy is the share count, which fell 2.7% in a year. ## Reasons to own Qualys

- A 53% free cash flow margin in the third quarter and about 43% for the year, with $808 million of cash and no borrowings that I could find.
- A record of beating its own guidance: the 2026 revenue range started at $717 million to $725 million and now stands at $736.9 million to $738.9 million.
- A shrinking share count, 2.7% fewer diluted shares in a year, funded from cash flow.
- Channel partners now produce 55% of revenue and grew 24%, which gives the company reach without a larger direct sales force.
- Automated repair of security weaknesses could deepen the product beyond scanning, which is the area competitors and AI tools are attacking.

## What could go wrong?

- **Growth is slowing:** billings grew 16% in the second quarter, 14% in the third, and about 8% is guided for the fourth. Revenue follows billings with a delay.
- **The federal quarter:** management called it unprecedented and said some deals could have slipped into the fourth quarter. If they did, the fourth quarter benefits; if they did not, the third quarter pulled sales forward.
- **Customer expansion is flat:** net dollar expansion of 105% is stable but modest. Revenue growth depends on new customers.
- **Direct sales are shrinking:** direct revenue fell 2% in the quarter while channel revenue grew 24%. The shift lowers selling cost per sale but also lowers control over the customer.
- **Competition and AI:** the filings list both. Security software stocks have sold off together this year on AI concerns, and I cannot estimate how much of that risk remains.
- **Valuation:** the multiple moved 120% in five months. A return toward the spring level would put the stock near or below my Bear value.
- **Accounting basis:** free cash flow before stock pay and a possible tax timing benefit overstate what belongs to shareholders. I adjust for both, but cash taxes are an estimate.

## Management execution

Management guided 2026 revenue growth of 7% to 8% in February and is now heading to about 10%, which makes the guidance conservative by roughly two points. Free cash flow reached 45% of revenue in 2025 and has been held near that level. The weaker side is spending: sales and marketing grew about 17% against revenue growth of 11%, and the non-GAAP operating margin slipped a point. The company has also bought back more than $1.2 billion of stock since 2018.

## Final verdict: hold at $172.51

Qualys has shown that it can turn a quarter of software sales into cash at a rate most companies cannot. The price after the drop still pays 23.1 times what is left after stock compensation, for a business that has guided its next billings quarter to about 8% growth. The Base earns 6.9% a year, which is less than I require, and a return to 23 times is needed to earn about 11%.

**Verdict: HOLD at $172.51, do not add. Base $148.70, -13.8% against the price, Bear $113.30, Bull $182.67, Confidence B (18.75/25). Required margin of safety 30%, maximum buy price $104.09, range $99.83 to $106.92 under the two methods.**

The buy zone can come to the stock in two ways: a price near $104.09, or evidence that billings and owner free cash flow are growing 8% to 10% with the margin intact, which would lift the Base. A fourth quarter that includes the deferred federal deals and still guides 2027 revenue growth near 8% would be the cleanest version of that. At 23 times owner free cash flow you are paying for the federal quarter to repeat, and management has already said it may not.
