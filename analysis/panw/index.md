## What Palo Alto Networks is

Palo Alto Networks sells cybersecurity as a platform: network and AI security (firewalls and cloud security), Cortex (security operations) and Idira, the identity platform the company reports alongside them. In the fourth-quarter release the company named strength across all three. It also owns Chronosphere, an observability business bought for $3.35 billion, and on November 18 it announced its intent to acquire Applied Compute to deliver secure open weight AI models; I did not find the price.

The key number the company reports is next-generation security annual recurring revenue (NGS ARR): $9.73 billion at October 31, up 66% from a year earlier. Remaining performance obligations (contracted revenue not yet recognized) were $21.0 billion, up 35%. The growth is not all organic. Goodwill rose from $4.57 billion to $22.0 billion in fiscal 2026 and to $22.56 billion at October 31, and weighted diluted shares rose from 709 million to 838 million in a year. The CFO said on the fourth-quarter call that last year's net new ARR included a nine-figure benefit from one large AI model customer moving to Chronosphere. The release does not split growth into organic and acquired, and I did not find the split elsewhere.

Guidance for fiscal 2027 (August 2026 to July 2027) was raised on November 18: revenue to $14.52 to $14.57 billion from $14.10 to $14.20 billion, NGS ARR to $11.475 to $11.525 billion, non-GAAP earnings a share to $4.26 to $4.30 from $4.16 to $4.19, with a 29.5% non-GAAP operating margin and adjusted free cash flow of 38% of sales.

## What could go wrong?

- Stock pay. $622 million in one quarter is 18.1% of sales and 58.7% of non-GAAP operating profit. If it does not fall toward 10% of sales, the Base is worth about $83 (2 points higher in every year) or less.
- Dilution. Weighted basic shares are up 21% in a year, mostly from CyberArk, and the company just announced another acquisition.
- Acquired growth. The fiscal 2027 guide of 27% includes a full year of CyberArk and Chronosphere; I could not find the organic rate, and last year's net new ARR had a nine-figure one-off from a single customer's migration.
- Gross margin. It fell from 74.2% to 68.0% in a year; the release does not say how much is acquired businesses and how much is hardware or hosting cost.
- The multiple. At 108 times my fiscal 2027 owner free cash flow, a fall to 60 times with no change in the business takes the shares to about $332 in 2031.
- Convertible notes. $1.93 billion moved from long-term to short-term; settlement in cash or shares is a use of the cash I count, and I did not check the terms.
- Cash quality. Part of the cash flow is prepayment: more than half of my fiscal 2027 owner free cash flow is working capital, which stops when growth slows.

## Management execution

Management has beaten its own quarterly guide and raised the year's guide, and it has absorbed two large acquisitions, CyberArk and Chronosphere. The offsets are the size of the stock pay, the share count that rose 18% in a year, and a reporting style that favors adjusted measures: the free cash flow measure adds back acquisition payments, and the operating margin leaves out stock pay. **Assessment: 13/20, good on execution and weaker on capital allocation.
