Qualcomm reported $8.65 billion of free cash flow for fiscal 2026, and $3.45 billion of stock compensation that the figure leaves out. Treat that stock as a cost and my main cash flow method values the shares at $110. Treat it as free and the same method gives $171, almost exactly the $165.94 the market pays. The whole debate about this stock is a debate about whether paying people in shares is a cost.

Valuation route: a chip designer without its own factories, plus a patent licensing arm, so it is valued on owner free cash flow, meaning free cash flow less stock compensation, and on earnings after stock compensation. Primary method: a five-year cash flow path with a terminal value, discounted at 10%. Cross-check: earnings in five years at an exit multiple, plus dividends. Both are tested against a reverse valuation. Values are present fair-value estimates, not 12-month price targets.

## What Qualcomm is

Qualcomm has two businesses. The chip segment (QCT) sells Snapdragon processors and modems for phones, cars and connected devices. The licensing segment (QTL) collects royalties on the patents behind modern wireless standards, and it earned a 72.4% pretax margin in fiscal 2026. Chips are the larger part of revenue and licensing is the larger part of the quality.

| Revenue ($ million) | Fiscal 2026 | Fourth quarter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chips for handsets | 24,072 | 5,137 |
| Chips for cars | 6,004 | 1,990 |
| Chips for connected devices (IoT) | 7,132 | 1,888 |
| Patent licensing (QTL) | 5,556 | 1,304 |
| Nonreportable segments (data center, government, Modular) and unallocated items | 356 | 3 |
| Total, accounting basis | 43,120 | 10,322 |

The fiscal 2026 figure of 356 is $476 million of nonreportable segments less $120 million of unallocated items, and fourth-quarter revenue is net of a $120 million legal settlement payment to a customer. Handset chips fell 13.4% over the year and 26.2% in the fourth quarter, and in that quarter they were 57% of chip revenue against 71% a year earlier. Car chips grew 51.7% over the year, and the fourth quarter at $1.99 billion, up 89%, was the first $2 billion automotive quarter by the company's own description. Handsets are being replaced by cars and, in the plan, by data centers.

## Owner free cash flow, the number I value

Qualcomm does not report free cash flow. I take operating cash flow less capital spending, which is the usual definition, and then subtract stock compensation, because the cash flow statement adds it back as a non-cash cost even though it is paid to employees in shares that dilute owners.

| Fiscal 2026 owner free cash flow | $ million | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating cash flow | 11,145 | reported |
| Capital spending | -2,494 | reported |
| Free cash flow | 8,651 | my calculation; $8.07 a share, down from $12.82 billion a year earlier |
| Stock compensation | -3,452 | reported; 8.0% of revenue and 40% of free cash flow |
| Owner free cash flow | 5,199 | $4.85 a share on 1,072 million shares |
| Inventory adjustment | +1,057 | my judgment: half of the $2,114 million rise in inventories reverses |
| Normalized owner free cash flow | 6,256 | $5.84 a share |

Inventories rose from $6.53 billion to $8.64 billion in a year in which revenue fell, which is a use of cash. The company says original equipment makers cut handset builds because of memory shortages, and that is the most likely reason stock built up. I credit back half of the increase. The Bear case credits none of it and the Bull case all of it. I did not verify cash taxes against the tax charge, which is distorted by the $5.7 billion valuation allowance release, so I rely on reported operating cash flow, which already contains the taxes actually paid.

Growth of 9% a year takes owner free cash flow to $8.98 a share in 2031, 54% above today's normalized level. That is generous for a company whose largest handset customer is leaving, and it needs automotive and the data center business to fill the gap that Apple modems leave, while memory and other input costs are passed on in prices. The 74% share of terminal value is below the 85% I treat as a prompt for extra checking on a five-year horizon, and I count the risk once, through the 10% rate. The 14.7 times terminal multiple is just what 3% growth at a 10% rate means. It is not a market price.

## Dividend and buybacks

The quarterly dividend is $0.92 a share, or $3.68 a year, a 2.2% yield, with the next ex-dividend date on December 3, 2026. Fiscal 2026 dividends of $3.85 billion took 74% of owner free cash flow, before any buyback. With the $7.82 billion of repurchases, total payouts were 2.2 times owner free cash flow.

The buyback record is mixed. In fiscal 2026 Qualcomm repurchased 48 million shares at an average of $161.96, close to today's price and 44% above my Base value. With about $19.6 billion of authorization left, the programme can continue for a long time, but at this price each dollar of buyback adds little to value in my model.

## Reasons to own Qualcomm

- The licensing business earns a 72.4% pretax margin on $5.56 billion of fiscal 2026 revenue. Long-term licenses were renewed with Apple, Huawei and Transsion in November, with royalty rates held according to management, and key licenses now run into fiscal 2030 to 2033.
- Automotive revenue grew 51.7% to $6.0 billion, and the fourth quarter at $1.99 billion was the first above $2 billion. Car chips are now 16% of chip revenue against 10% a year earlier.
- The company reiterated a fiscal 2027 data center revenue target of $5 billion and announced a multi-generational collaboration with Amazon for AI data centers. Non-handset revenue is projected to grow by more than 60% next year.
- Net debt of $7.4 billion is below one year of free cash flow, and the new notes did not raise it.
- On adjusted earnings the stock sits at 17.1 times, in the lower half of its own range of 12.6 to 26.8 times.

## What could go wrong?

- Apple leaves faster. The annual report now says Apple will primarily use its own modems, where a year earlier it said increasingly, and management said modem share in the next iPhone would be well below 20%. Apple revenue is guided to fall about 50% in one quarter and to less than $2 billion in fiscal 2027. The two largest customers were 22% and 20% of revenue.
- China is 42% of revenue, and Samsung and Xiaomi are building more of their own chips.
- Memory and input costs. Original equipment makers cut handset builds because of memory constraints, gross margin fell from 55.4% to 53.1% over the year and to 50.7% in the fourth quarter, and Qualcomm expects the full benefit of its price increases only in the second half of fiscal 2027.
- Inventories rose 32% to $8.64 billion while revenue fell 3%. If demand does not recover, a write-down is possible.
- The data center business is unproven. The group of segments that holds it earned $476 million of revenue and lost $607 million before tax in fiscal 2026, and the Amazon warrant allows up to 25 million new shares at $161.26.
- Legal and accounting items. A jury found on October 13 that Arm breached Qualcomm's architecture license, an appeal ruling is pending, and no accrual is recorded. Goodwill is $16.4 billion.

## Management execution

- Guidance was met. Fourth-quarter adjusted earnings of $2.17 sat inside the guided range of $2.05 to $2.25, and revenue was at the high end. The first-quarter guide of $2.15 to $2.35 has a midpoint below the consensus of $2.37, and the stock fell for it.
- The Apple modem exit, long expected, is now quantified in the guidance. The test is the replacement revenue, and cars have delivered so far, with 89% growth in the fourth quarter.
- Qualcomm bought Alphawave for about $2.4 billion, closing in December 2025, a quarter ahead of schedule, and bought Modular in the third quarter. Alphawave was bought to build the data center business.
- Capital allocation returns cash whatever the price: payouts of 135% of free cash flow, a $4.0 billion note issue in November, and buybacks near $162 a share. Payouts at this rate depend on cash on hand and borrowing.

## What I would watch from here

### Green

- Gross margin moves back toward 55% as the price increases take effect in the second half of fiscal 2027.
- Data center shows up as its own revenue line from the first quarter of fiscal 2027 and tracks toward the $5 billion target.
- Inventories fall below $8 billion without a write-down, and the price drifts toward $110 while cash flow holds.

### Yellow

- Handset revenue falls by more than the guided mid single digit percentage in the first quarter.
- Inventories keep rising while revenue falls.
- Payouts stay above free cash flow and net debt starts to rise.

### Red

- The data center target is cut or pushed out of fiscal 2027.
- Another large customer announces a full switch to its own modem or chips.
- The Arm appeal goes against Qualcomm, or licensing revenue turns down.

The next hard data are the December quarter Apple revenue fall, the first quarter results in early 2027 and the ex-dividend date on December 3.

## Final verdict: reduce at $165.94

Qualcomm is a better business than its stock chart suggests and a worse bet than its multiple suggests. The licensing stream is secure into the 2030s, the cars are growing and the balance sheet is fine. The price asks for all of that, plus a data center business, and plus a view that $3.45 billion of annual stock pay is not a cost.
