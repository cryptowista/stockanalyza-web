## What AST SpaceMobile is

AST SpaceMobile is building a network of large satellites, called BlueBirds, that connect directly to ordinary phones, so a customer of a mobile carrier gets signal where there are no towers. Its customers are mobile carriers and governments, not consumers.

- Revenue was $31.5 million in Q2 and $115.3 million over the last twelve months, but most of it is not service revenue. In Q2, $24.4 million came from products (gateways and similar equipment) and $7.1 million from services.
- The company says 13 satellites are in orbit, 3 more are in final testing and production runs through BlueBird 46. It targets about 45 in orbit by early 2027 and says commercial service could start with as few as 45.
- It reports more than 60 carrier partners covering over 3 billion subscribers, a revenue backlog of about $1.3 billion and more than $100 million of funded US government awards. The revenue-sharing terms with carriers are not disclosed.
- Spectrum is the other asset: up to 45 MHz of lower mid-band in North America through Ligado (announced in June 2025: about $550 million at closing, then quarterly usage payments that started at $16 million and rise 3% a year, and the 10-Q shows a further $100 million capital advance in 2026), plus carrier-owned low and mid-band spectrum on the satellites.
- The main competitor is now much larger. SpaceX listed its shares in June and, after the market closed on Thursday, agreed to buy spectrum from Grain Management (up to 14 MHz in the 800 MHz band, according to press reports). The deal still needs regulatory approval, and AST has said nothing about it so far.

I use 2028 revenue because it is the first full year of commercial service. ## Reasons to own ASTS

- **Funding.** Pro forma liquidity is more than $3.7 billion including restricted cash, the debt carries coupons of 1.625% to 4.25% and no convertible note matures before 2032.
- **Spectrum and partners.** Rights to up to 45 MHz of lower mid-band in North America and more than 60 carrier partners are hard to copy, and AT&T and Verizon are partners in the US.
- **Backlog and government.** About $1.3 billion of backlog, more than $100 million of funded US government awards, and a preliminary Japanese program with Rakuten worth up to about $1 billion in government capital, subject to approvals.
- **Manufacturing pace.** Production is at about 6 satellites a month, and 6 BlueBirds were launched within 50 days.
- **Lower cost of capital.** The July notes priced at 1.625% with a conversion premium of 20% and a capped call to $149.20.

## What could go wrong?

- **Competition.** SpaceX is a public company with its own satellites and launches, and it is buying low-band spectrum. If the large carriers turn to Starlink Mobile, the carrier model that AST depends on loses its edge. Verizon and AT&T shares fell 8.75% and 9.81% on Friday, according to press reports.
- **Funding and dilution.** My Base needs more capital before 2031, and any new raise is at a share price that has fallen 62% from its 52-week high.
- **Launch risk.** One satellite was lost in April, and the plan needs a launch every one to two months from two providers.
- **Commercial timing.** Revenue depends on integration with carriers and government milestones. Revenue-sharing terms and service gross margin are not disclosed.
- **Terminal value dependence.** More than the whole secondary value is after 2032.
- **Control and trading.** One holder controls the vote, short interest is 24.3% of the float and the five-year beta is 2.71.

## Management execution

Management has issued $4.4 billion of convertible notes since January 2025 on terms most pre-revenue companies cannot get, reiterated the 2026 revenue range of $150 million to $200 million, and met the Q2 capital spending guide. Against that, the target of 45 satellites in orbit changed from 2026, as confirmed in May, to early 2027 in August, one satellite was lost, adjusted operating expenses came in just above the guided range, and the revenue-sharing terms and the gross margin of commercial service are not disclosed. On the Q2 call the company described the July notes as "effective dilution of less than 2%". That holds inside the capped call, which covers prices up to $149.20; the notes themselves convert into 14.45 million shares above $79.57 and are $1.15 billion of debt until then. Management credibility scores **11/20**.

## Final verdict: sell at $50.97

AST SpaceMobile has funding, spectrum, partners and a real satellite factory. It also has $115 million of revenue, a burn of $1.64 billion a year and a share count that grew 19.6% in twelve months. At my Base the network is worth about what it costs to finish it. At my Bull, with the company's target met and exceeded, it is worth $31.70. The price needs more than that. Because equity is only a thin residual of the enterprise value here, read the range from Bear to Bull rather than the Base alone. Verdict: SELL at $50.97. Triangulated Base value $3.00, downside 94.1%, Confidence D (8/25). Required margin of safety above the 60% ceiling, no buy zone. The case changes if commercial service starts on schedule at or above the $1 billion first-year target, if the carriers commit to AST in a way that SpaceX cannot undo, or if the next financing is non-dilutive. The 45th satellite is a launch schedule. The first quarter of commercial service revenue is the number that will show what the first 45 were worth, and it has not been reported yet.
