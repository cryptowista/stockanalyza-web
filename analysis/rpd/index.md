## What Rapid7 is

Rapid7 sells security software and managed services that detect and respond to attacks (detection and response, including managed detection and response) and that find weak points in a customer's systems (exposure management). The company describes itself as a provider of AI-powered managed cybersecurity operations. It has approximately 11,800 customers.

The business is splitting in two. Core platform solutions are 85% of ARR and grew 1% year over year; detection and response is more than 55% of ARR and grows about 5% a year (Motley Fool call transcript on Webull). Exposure management ARR fell about 6% and non-core ARR about 19%, and ARR per customer slipped to about $69,000 from $72,000 two quarters earlier (Earnings Whispers). Management called the third quarter an early sign of stability and said one quarter is not a trend.

The leadership is new. Wael Mohamed, described by Barchart as the newly appointed chief executive, announced a 12% workforce cut in the second quarter, with $10 million to $11 million of restructuring charges. The 10-Q says four directors resigned effective August 27, 2026, and that the resignations were not due to any disagreement with the company.

## Reasons to own Rapid7

- Cost cuts are working: the non-GAAP operating margin was 21.7% in the third quarter, and the third-quarter profit of $0.60 a share beat the guide of $0.44 to $0.47.
- The core is stabilizing: core ARR held flat in the quarter, as promised, and detection and response grows about 5% and is more than half of ARR.
- Cash is $731.6 million against $900 million of notes with no revolver draw, and free cash flow guided at about $130 million.

- Valuation is low on adjusted numbers: 8.3 times guided non-GAAP EPS.

## What could go wrong?

- Shrinking ARR. ARR is down 2.2% and guided down 3% for December, and ARR per customer is falling. A company that shrinks can be worth less than its cash flow suggests.
- Stock pay. At about 10% of sales it is a real cost of the business; 2 points of sales higher in every year takes the Base to $12.01.
- The 2027 maturity. The $600 million due March 15, 2027 will be paid from cash, which removes interest income of roughly $20 million a year from reported cash flow.
- Dilution. Unvested units total 15.2 million, 22% of shares outstanding.
- Tax dispute. About $105 million assessed by the Israel Tax Authority, with no impact recorded.
- Leadership churn. A new chief executive, a 12% workforce cut and four directors who resigned on one day in August.
- No outlook for 2027. Management has not said where sales and ARR go next year.
