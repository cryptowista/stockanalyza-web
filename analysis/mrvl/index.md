## What Marvell is

Marvell designs the chips that move, switch, store and process data in data centers: custom AI accelerators and related chips for hyperscalers, electrical and optical interconnect (the DSPs and drivers inside high speed links), Ethernet switching, and storage controllers. It does not make chips itself; foundries do. The Data Center segment was $2.17 billion of the $2.74 billion second quarter revenue (79%, up from 74% a year earlier) and grew 46%. The rest, Communications and other, grew 10%.

Two acquisitions changed the shape of the company this year. Celestial AI, a photonic interconnect developer, closed on February 2, 2026 for about $1.3 billion in cash and about 24.5 million shares, with more cash and shares payable through fiscal 2029 if revenue milestones are met. XConn Technologies closed on February 10 for about $280 million in cash and 2.1 million shares. First-half acquisition outlays net of cash acquired were $1,270.9 million. Goodwill and acquired intangibles now total $16.2 billion, 59% of total assets and 88% of equity.

The other change is Google. In late July 2026 Marvell signed an expanded custom chip agreement with Google and issued it a warrant, disclosed on August 18, for 58,970,907 shares at $206.58, expiring August 18, 2033. A first slice of 1,360,867 shares vests over the first year. The other 57,610,040 shares vest one tranche per $500 million of custom product revenue Marvell recognizes from Google, from the third quarter of fiscal 2027 through fiscal 2033. Google pays $206.58 a share on exercise, so full exercise would bring in $12.2 billion and would require $120 billion of purchases. The purchases are at Google's discretion.

I could not verify customer concentration percentages for fiscal 2027, and the first draft gave none. The release itself warns that major customers are an increasing share of revenue.

## Balance sheet and shares

Net debt is **$1,030 million**: $4,962.9 million of long-term debt less $3,932.8 million of cash. The first draft listed cash and borrowings but never used them in a bridge. I leave out operating leases, because rent is already inside operating cash flow. Two debt-like items are not quantified in the figures I could verify and are excluded from the bridge: the contingent consideration liability for Celestial and XConn (revalued upward by $433.7 million in the first half) and the forward stock purchase contract. Each $1 billion of such items would cost $1.05 a share.

The share count has three layers. Basic weighted shares of 897.4 million already include NVIDIA's $2.0 billion Series A convertible preferred stock as if converted, because Marvell reports under the two-class method: common shares outstanding are 876.9 million, which implies about 20 million preferred-equivalent shares. The 921 million diluted figure includes it too, so the first draft's list of additional dilution that named the preferred conversion counted it twice. What is genuinely extra is the Google warrant (14.2 million shares at today's price by the treasury method, up to 59.0 million shares if fully vested) and the Celestial and XConn earn-outs, which are payable partly in shares. I use **950 million economic shares** for all per share values: 921 million, plus about 14 million for the warrant, plus about 15 million for earn-out shares and award growth. The last part is my estimate.

Buybacks do not offset it. Marvell spent $400 million on repurchases in the first half but also paid $365.2 million of tax on employee share settlements and $107.7 million of dividends. The dividend is $0.06 a quarter, a yield of 0.09%, with the next ex-dividend date on October 9, 2026.

## Primary valuation: a multiple of free cash flow per share

Fiscal 2027 consensus free cash flow of $2.65 billion divided by 950 million shares is **$2.79 a share**. The first draft gave a Base of about $140 with no inputs. That value equals a multiple of 50x on this figure, so I use 50x as an explicit assumption. It is not the output of a build: Marvell is growing fast and has a strong design pipeline, which supports a multiple well above a mature chip company's 20x to 25x, but I cannot derive 50x from first principles. ## Secondary valuation: ten-year free cash flow model

## What is the market already pricing in?

At $272.29 and 950 million economic shares, market value is about $258.7 billion and enterprise value about **$259.7 billion**. At my 50x multiple the price needs fiscal 2027 free cash flow of about **$5.2 billion**, 95% above consensus. In the model, the price equals the Base path at roughly twice the cash flow in every year (fiscal 2028 near $7.6 billion instead of $3.8 billion), or the Base path at a discount rate of about 6.3%. The full consensus path through fiscal 2029, with growth continuing after it, needs a discount rate of about 8.2%.

A buyer today needs consensus to be met and then beaten: custom silicon ramping to its full potential, connectivity staying in short supply, gross margin holding near 58%, the Celestial and XConn investments paying back, and the Google warrant being earned in full, which means very large purchases. The price is not asking for a good outcome. It is asking for a result above the analysts' plan.

## Reasons to own MRVL

- **Broad exposure to AI infrastructure.** Custom silicon, interconnect, switching and storage are all sold to the same small group of buyers.
- **Deep hyperscaler design relationships.** Custom programs take years to design in and years to replace, and the Google agreement now ties a customer's purchases to equity.
- **Accelerating revenue.** Data Center revenue grew 46%, and the Q3 outlook is above 50% growth for the company.
- **New connectivity capability.** Celestial AI and XConn broaden the optical and scale-up portfolio.
- **Very large absolute cash flow potential.** Management's $18 billion fiscal 2028 revenue outlook would multiply free cash flow if margins hold.

## What could go wrong?

- **Thesis breaker: free cash flow per share fails to scale fast enough.** Free cash flow was flat in fiscal 2026 on revenue up 42%, and a repeat is the central risk to a price that needs it to double.
- **Valuation.** At 98 times fiscal 2027 consensus free cash flow, good execution can still produce weak returns. The 52 week high is $329.88 and the shares are 17% below it.
- **Customer concentration.** The company warns that major customers are a growing share of revenue, and custom programs can be delayed or lost.
- **SBC and dilution.** SBC was 11.9% of revenue in the latest quarter, diluted shares are up 5.8% in a year, and the Google warrant covers up to 59.0 million more.
- **Acquisition risk.** Goodwill and intangibles are $16.2 billion, and the earn-outs for Celestial and XConn are payable in cash and shares through fiscal 2029.
- **Supply and cycle.** Foundry capacity, product ramps and hyperscaler capital spending can all move away from consensus.

## Management execution

Management has beaten its revenue guidance by a small margin in each recent quarter, raised its outlook twice in a row and signed a long custom agreement with Google. The offsets are a record of paying for growth with shares, a first-half earn-out revaluation of $433.7 million, and per share cash generation that did not grow in fiscal 2026. **Assessment: 16/20, good. The next test is the Investor Day on October 6, 2026, where management will present its long term targets.**

## Final verdict: reduce at $272.29

Marvell is a high-quality AI semiconductor business with accelerating revenue, a new anchor agreement with Google and a record of raising its own outlook. The shares, though, trade 97% above my Base and above my Bull, and they need free cash flow per share to roughly double against a base that did not grow last year. Google can buy the stock at $206.58 if it keeps buying the chips. At $272.29 the rest of us would be paying 32% more for the same shares.

**Verdict: REDUCE at $272.29. Triangulated Base value $138.20, downside 49.2%, Confidence C (13/25). Required margin of safety 50%, maximum buy price $69.**

The case changes if free cash flow per share starts compounding faster than SBC and the share count, if the October 6 Investor Day raises the long-term targets above the consensus path, or if the price falls toward the buy zone with the business still executing.
