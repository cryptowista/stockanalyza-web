## What Netskope is

Netskope sells security and networking delivered from the cloud: it sits between employees and the web, cloud apps and AI tools, and decides what data may pass. The category is called secure service edge (SSE) or SASE, and Netskope competes in it with Zscaler and Palo Alto Networks, among others. Customers run their traffic through the company's own network, NewEdge, which has 80 regions. Sanjay Beri is chief executive. The company was founded as Skope, Inc., changed its name in November 2012, listed on Nasdaq in September 2025 and had 3,281 employees at January 31, 2026, before a June 2026 plan to cut about 5% of the workforce.

The numbers show a company that is growing quickly and is still paying for it. Revenue was $709 million in fiscal 2026 and the guide for fiscal 2027 is $888 million to $892 million, up about 26%. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) is $899 million, up 27%, with the Americas up 25%, EMEA up 37% and Asia-Pacific and Japan up 31%. Remaining performance obligations (RPO, contracted revenue not yet recognized) are about $1.4 billion, up 36%, with 53% due within 12 months. Dollar-based net retention is 114%, down from 118% a year ago, and 59% of customers use four or more products.

The cost side is heavy. On a GAAP basis, research and development alone is 46% of sales (Shashi Bellamkonda), and the GAAP operating loss was $89.8 million in the quarter, 41% of sales. Gross margin is improving as the network build ends: 74% GAAP and 77% non-GAAP against 72% and 75% a year earlier.
