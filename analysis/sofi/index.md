# SoFi Technologies: real growth, but returns on capital still have to be earned

SOFI, NASDAQ. Published September 22, 2026. Price as of 3:26 pm EDT on September 22, 2026 (regular session).

## Verdict: HOLD at $17.16 (watchlist for new money)

Watchlist for new money, conditional hold for existing holders. Growth is real, but the price sits above Base value while returns on the enlarged capital base remain below the cost of equity.

- Bear value: $3.90
- Base value: $14.84 (downside 13.5%)

SoFi's growth is real. In Q2 2026, members grew 35% and GAAP revenue 43%, and management kept its FY2026 earnings guidance despite a much higher tax assumption. The harder question is whether that growth turns into durable returns on tangible common equity above the cost of equity, without repeated external capital or understated credit costs.

At $17.16 on September 22, 2026, the shares trade 15.6% above my present Base value of $14.84. The capital-based primary model values SoFi below the market, while the independent earnings model lands almost exactly on the price. SoFi is neither an obviously broken business nor an obvious bargain, and a Confidence C valuation needs a large discount before new money makes sense.

Route: SF-02 Banks, digital bank holding company with lending, financial services and technology platform operations. Economic classification: Quality Growth (7/12), not a demonstrated compounder. Primary method: growth-adjusted justified P/TBV through a staged 10-year residual income model. Secondary method: revenue times normalized net margin times forward P/E. All values are present values per economic share, not future price targets.

## Why SoFi is valued as a bank

SoFi operates a regulated bank inside a financial holding structure, funds interest-earning assets with deposits and bears material consumer credit exposure. Its fee and technology businesses can improve capital efficiency, but they do not make consolidated SoFi a subscription software company.

The hard economic measures are common earnings after normal taxes, credit costs and compensation, tangible common equity (TCE), return on tangible common equity (ROTCE), funding quality and regulatory capital. Corporate free cash flow, EV/EBITDA, EV/revenue, SaaS multiples and the company's Rule of 40 presentation are not used. Deposits are operating funding, not debt to deduct, and consolidated cash is not automatically distributable.

Q2 adjusted EBITDA of $357.8 million is not owner-distributable bank income. H1 operating cash flow of minus $6.2 billion reflects held-for-sale loan funding, not a software-style cash burn. Neither number anchors the valuation.

## A changing business

Fee businesses and the Loan Platform Business can grow revenue without keeping every loan on the balance sheet, which is potentially capital-efficient growth. At the same time, retained lending is expanding fast: personal loans are about 58.9% of unpaid principal in the fair-value loan portfolio. The 2025 capital raises increased capacity to support that growth, which makes proof of returns on the enlarged capital base essential.

Technology Platform revenue fell 23% year over year after the loss of a large client, despite a sequential recovery. Segment contribution margins also declined: Financial Services to 46% from 52%, Technology Platform to 14% from 30%. A high consolidated growth rate does not prove every segment has a durable moat.

On September 22, 2026, SoFi announced it had gone live with stablecoin settlement across Mastercard's global network. It is a genuine product milestone, but payment volume is not SoFi revenue and no separate stablecoin premium is added to the Base value.

## Credit and the fair-value loan book

The personal loan portfolio is carried at fair value, so expected credit economics sit in valuation marks and related income. The Financial Services provision is not the total loss cost of the consolidated personal loan business. Fair-value marks, delinquent loan sales and realized charge-offs have to be read together, not added mechanically.

Three different loss measures exist: management's all-in personal loan charge-off estimate including delinquent loan sales of about 3.7%, the reported portfolio charge-off rate of 2.62%, and the 4.77% annual default assumption in the fair-value disclosure. None should be mislabeled as another.

The all-loan fair-value premium of $2,027.1 million equals about 21.4% of reported TCE. That is a valuation-sensitive exposure, not proof the asset is worthless. An extra 100 basis points of annual loss on $26.1 billion of personal loan principal costs about $195.8 million after tax, or $0.142 per Base share per year.

## Primary valuation: residual income on tangible equity

A bank is worth its tangible common capital plus the present value of profits earned above the return required on that capital. SoFi is not valued as an immediately mature 20% to 30% ROTCE franchise. Each year, net income equals beginning economic TCE times normalized ROTCE, and residual income equals net income less the cost of equity on beginning capital. The horizon covers ten rolling years from September 2026 to September 2036.

## Bear, base and bull scenarios

**Bear:** lending and fee growth slow, credit normalization absorbs more revenue, and returns stay below the cost of equity despite continued profits. No rescue equity is assumed, and more severe regulatory or liquidity distress could produce values below the Bear case. **Base:** fee monetization and operating leverage improve, credit losses stay manageable and the enlarged capital base is deployed gradually, with returns rising above the cost of equity but short of management's full objective. **Bull:** fee mix and operating leverage support a high-return platform, with returns approaching the lower or middle part of management's ambition. There is no separate crypto or stablecoin value in any case.

## Reasons to own or follow SOFI

- **Integrated distribution.** The reported existing-member cross-buy share was 51%, supporting a credible distribution advantage. The test is higher profit per customer, not simply more counted products.
- **Deposit funding.** A predominantly deposit-funded platform can earn better economics than one reliant on warehouse facilities, if retention does not need uneconomic pricing.
- **Capital-light fees.** Loan platform, interchange, brokerage and service fees can raise revenue per unit of tangible capital.
- **Execution.** The 2025 revenue and EBITDA outperformance and the tax-adjusted 2026 guidance upgrade support management's delivery record.
- **An identifiable Bull mechanism.** If margins and capital efficiency approach management's long-term objectives, value can materially exceed today's price.

## What could go wrong?

- **Credit and true earnings.** Rapid growth and delinquent loan sales can make a single loss ratio misleading, and fair-value assumptions must reconcile with realized credit. Three years of 100 basis points of extra personal loan losses cut the Base value to about $13.84.
- **Capital and liquidity.** Capital is healthy but being consumed, and parent-level cash access and the October 2026 note maturity matter separately from consolidated liquidity.
- **Dilution and capital allocation.** Further dilution, technology spending or acquisitions may add revenue without per-share returns. 5% more economic shares lower the Base value to $14.13.
- **Regulation and execution.** Consumer lending, deposit competition, third-party loan demand and newer payment activities face changing economic and compliance demands.

## Adversarial review

The arithmetic review recomputed the capital and share bridges, every scenario, the terminal multiples, weights, reverse solutions, sensitivities, margin of safety and scores. A separate high-precision implementation revalued the distributions and terminal cash flows without reusing the residual income function. The clean re-audit passed 165 consistency checks and 23 separate precision checks.

The economic review prevented SoFi from being routed as a SaaS company or valued on industrial free cash flow, reconciled issued, diluted and adjusted share counts, added option exercise cash alongside option shares, avoided counting future compensation twice, replaced an unverifiable book value input with the reported tangible book series, recognized the tax-adjusted guidance upgrade, discounted each future horizon to today, separated capital ratios from parent liquidity and gave the stablecoin launch no value without incremental economics.

The strongest counter-thesis is that SoFi can earn 20% to 30% ROTCE, grow fee revenue with less capital and put the raised capital to productive use. The secondary Base already supports about $17.13 and the full Bull case $34.87. The no-new-money conclusion does not survive every plausible Bull outcome. It stands because the evidence does not yet make those outcomes the central case.
