## What ADM is

ADM buys, stores, transports and processes crops. Its largest segment, Agriculture Services and Oilseeds, trades grain, runs the storage and shipping network, crushes oilseeds into meal and oil and makes refined oils and biodiesel, and it includes ADM's equity stake in Wilmar. Carbohydrate Solutions processes corn into sweeteners, starches and ethanol. Nutrition makes ingredients for people and animals. Total revenue was $22.3 billion in the third quarter, but segment operating profit was only $1.4 billion, because this is a thin-margin business.

Third-quarter segment profit included about $35 million of net positive mark-to-market and timing effects, and Other Business earnings included a one-time insurance reserve release that management put at $40 to $50 million.

## Cash flow, working capital and debt

Cash from operations before working capital was about $3.0 billion for the nine months against $2.1 billion a year earlier. Reported cash from operations was only $1.79 billion against $5.77 billion, because working capital absorbed $1.23 billion this year after releasing $3.68 billion last year. Operating working capital was $9.68 billion at September 30 against $7.18 billion at the end of 2025, and the working capital build is the largest difference between earnings and cash this year. Capital spending was $773 million, and the 2026 guide was cut to $1.2 to $1.3 billion.

Total debt was $8.74 billion and cash and marketable securities $0.76 billion, so net debt was about $7.98 billion, up from about $6.62 billion at the end of 2025. Short-term debt rose to $1.16 billion from $0.25 billion. Management reports net leverage of 1.5 times, and on my net debt and trailing adjusted EBITDA of $4.78 billion I get 1.7 times. The reported cash and restricted cash of $5.74 billion includes restricted funds, largely at ADM's brokerage subsidiary, so I do not count them.

In the nine months ADM paid $764 million of dividends and repurchased $358 million of stock. Diluted shares were 484 million, which I use, and adjusted return on invested capital was 9.0% over the trailing four quarters. Noncontrolling interests were not disclosed as a balance, so my bridge adds $293 million of temporary equity as other claims.

## Primary valuation: mid-cycle EV/EBITDA

I normalize EBITDA at $4.3 billion. The trailing four quarters are $4.78 billion, full-year 2025 was $3.7 billion and 2024 was $4.5 billion, so $4.3 billion is the average of the three. I do not take the trailing figure because it contains a year of unusually strong crush and biofuel margins, a $350 million expected 45Z benefit that depends on policy, and a one-time insurance release.

I apply 8.5 times, the lower end of the 8 to 12 times range I use for a moderately cyclical business. Adjusted return on invested capital is 9%, close to the cost of capital, earnings fell 28% in 2025 and have risen sharply since, working capital is large and net leverage is 1.7 times. At 8.5 times the equity is worth $28.3 billion, an owner cash flow yield of 6.7%, which at a 9.0% cost of equity implies growth of about 2.3% a year, in line with my second model.

The model gives **$58.42**: EV of $36.6 billion less net debt of $7.98 billion and other claims of $0.29 billion, divided by 484 million shares. The normalized EBITDA and the multiple are my estimates.

## Secondary valuation: owner cash flow

The second method values the cash left for shareholders before working capital swings. I normalize cash from operations before working capital at $3.4 billion, below the roughly $4 billion that 2026 is heading to (about $3.0 billion in nine months), subtract capital spending of $1.4 billion, above the 2026 guide because I want to cover maintenance and some growth, and subtract $0.1 billion for stock compensation, my estimate. Owner cash flow is $3.93 a share.

I grow it 3% a year for five years, 2.5% for years 6 to 10 and 2% after that, and discount at 9.0%. The model gives **$60.65**. Terminal value is 52% of it over a ten year horizon, below my 75% attention level. The method ignores working capital, so it overstates cash if the business keeps growing its inventories and receivables.

## Valuation triangulation

The two methods give $58.42 and $60.65, a divergence of 3.7%, well below the 20% warning level. They share the same view of normalized profit, so agreement is not independent evidence. I weight the EV/EBITDA method 60% and the cash flow model 40%, because the first captures the cycle in the multiple and the second does not capture working capital. The weighted value is $59.31.

## Reasons to own

- Segment operating profit rose 64% in the quarter, and management expects every segment to beat last year in the fourth quarter.
- Management expects 2027 to be broadly similar to 2026, so the new earnings level is not presented as a one-off.
- The company is on track for $500 to $750 million of cost savings, capital spending was cut and buybacks resumed.
- The dividend has grown for 53 straight years and is covered by normalized owner cash flow.

## What could go wrong?

- Biofuel policy. A large part of the jump rests on the March blending requirement and a 45Z credit worth about $350 million this year. Normalized EBITDA of $4.0 billion gives a Base value of $52.96.
- Margin peak. The CEO said third-quarter crush margins may have been the high point, although he expects strong margins in the fourth quarter and into 2027.
- Working capital. It rose by $2.5 billion in nine months, and net debt rose by about $1.4 billion with it.
- Multiple. A multiple of 8.0 times gives a Base value of $56.65.
- Interest costs. Management expects them to rise in 2027.

## Management execution

The numbers moved the right way: guidance rose from $3.60 to $4.25 in February to $5.50 to $5.80, capital spending guidance fell and leverage is 1.5 times by the company's measure. Against that, the improvement came mainly from policy and prices that management does not control, and cash from operations after working capital was lower than a year ago. The fourth-quarter report will show how much of the working capital build reverses.
