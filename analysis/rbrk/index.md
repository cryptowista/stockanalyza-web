## What Rubrik is

Rubrik sells software that backs up company data, finds ransomware and other damage inside it and recovers the data and the identity systems it depends on; the company calls itself a security and AI operations company. Cloud ARR, the part sold on its cloud platform, is $1.48 billion, which is 89% of subscription ARR, and grew 39%. There are 3,084 customers paying $100,000 or more in ARR, up 23% from a year earlier. It competes with Commvault, Veeam and Cohesity, and with the backup tools of the large cloud providers.

The business model is the reason cash flow runs ahead of profit. Billings run ahead of revenue, so deferred revenue is $1.18 billion current and $750.2 million non-current, about $1.93 billion in all, and the 10-Q puts remaining contracted revenue at about $2.62 billion, with about 53% to be recognized within 12 months. The same growth has a cost: stock-based pay of $101.0 million in the second quarter was 23.6% of sales.

The company is led by Bipul Sinha, chief executive, chairman and co-founder, with Kiran Choudary as chief financial officer. It has two classes of shares: 167.4 million Class A and 39.9 million Class B were outstanding on August 24, 2026. The United States is 68% of revenue.

- Stock pay. At about 22% of sales it exceeds free cash flow; it rose from $73.4 million in the first quarter to $101.0 million in the second, and 2 points of sales higher in every year takes the Base to $27.34.
- Slowing revenue. Third-quarter revenue is guided within about 1% of the second quarter, and the full-year guide implies about 3% sequential growth in the fourth.
- Competition. Commvault, Veeam, Cohesity and the cloud providers all sell backup and recovery.
- Dilution. Unvested units total 21.7 million, 10.5% of shares outstanding, and the 39.9 million Class B shares (19% of the total) are a separate class from the public Class A shares.
- The notes. $1.15 billion of convertible notes become 9.2 million shares if the price is above about $124.75 at conversion, and are debt below it.
- Data gaps. Employer payroll tax on stock pay, the capped call cap and a peer table were not available to me.
