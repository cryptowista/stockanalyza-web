On October 9, ten months after it refinanced its bank facilities, Universal amended the credit agreement to lift its leverage cap from 3.00 times to 3.75 times. At $33.02 the dividend of $3.32 yields 10.1%, and the shares are 44% below their February high of $59.38.

## What Universal is

Universal sources flue-cured, burley and dark air-cured tobacco from farmers in more than 30 countries, grades, processes and ships it to makers of consumer tobacco products. Tobacco Operations earned $211.5 million of operating income on $2,576.4 million of revenue in fiscal 2026, an 8.2% margin. Ingredients, which makes plant-based ingredients including fruit and vegetable products, earned $3.2 million on $348.1 million of revenue, down from $12.3 million a year earlier, and had a $41.1 million goodwill impairment at its Shank's operation in the fourth quarter.

The business is a service to a small group of large cigarette makers and a working capital machine. Customers commit to volumes, and the company holds the leaf in between. The risk is leaf it has bought and nobody has ordered: uncommitted inventory was 27% of tobacco inventory at March 31, 2026, above the 10% to 20% range management targets, and tobacco inventory write-downs were $43.4 million in fiscal 2026, against $18.7 million the year before and a five-year average the CFO put at about $14 million.

## Dividend

The quarterly dividend is $0.83, or $3.32 a year, a 10.1% yield at $33.02. In May 2026 the board raised it from $0.82 for the 56th consecutive year, an increase of one cent. It costs about $83 million a year on 24.9 million shares, and first-half dividends were $41.4 million. Free cash flow covered it once in the last five fiscal years, in the exceptional fiscal 2025, and fell $1.0 million short in fiscal 2026. On my Base path cash flow after dividends is negative every year to 2032. The record is an asset a board protects, and protecting it with borrowing has a limit that the covenant sets. A yield above 10% usually means the market doubts the payout, and I give the dividend no weight in the valuation beyond the cash it returns in the model.

## Reasons to own UVV

- **The cycle may be turning.** Worldwide unsold flue-cured and burley stocks fell by about 45 million kilograms in the quarter, and management expects smaller crops next season and says El Niño worries are drawing in new demand.
- **Customers have committed.** Management says commitments are in line with its full-year sales plan, and that 81% of tobacco inventory is committed.
- **The equity is a leveraged bet on recovery.** My Bull value of $42.07 is 27% above the price, and at 8.0x the multiple method alone gives $32.22.
- **The dividend history is long and the yield is high.** A record of 56 increases and a 10.1% yield mean income investors have reasons to hold, and a $100 million buyback authorization runs to November 2028.
- **Ingredients could stop losing money.** The restructuring is expected to run through fiscal 2028, and goodwill is down to $75.0 million after the impairment.

## What could go wrong?

- **The covenant gets tight.** The cap is 3.75 times for September and December 2026, 3.25 times for March 2027 and 3.00 times from June 2027. The first half cash outflow was $213.8 million, and failing a test could put access to the revolver at risk.
- **The dividend is cut or funded by debt.** On my Base path leverage stays near 4.3 times because cash after dividends is negative, and the board has to choose between the record and the balance sheet.
- **Oversupply lasts.** If EBITDA stays near $200 million my Bear value is $5.14, and with EBITDA 10% below Base the triangulated value is $15.81.
- **More write-downs.** Tobacco inventory write-downs were $43.4 million in fiscal 2026 against a five-year average of about $14 million, and uncommitted inventory was still $229.5 million in September.
- **Higher taxes and interest.** The tax rate was 34.3% in the first half against 25.2%, and at a 35% rate in the model the Base value is $21.03.
- **The core category shrinks.** Cigarette volumes fall over time, and the customers are a small group of large cigarette makers.

## Management execution
